# Question bank: Behavioral

Tags: `collaboration` `code-review` `debugging-stories`

Use the STAR shape (Situation, Task, Action, Result) as a guide, not a
script — a natural conversational answer that hits those beats is better
than a rigid recitation.

---

### Tell me about a time you disagreed with a code review comment on a React PR. What happened?

- **Difficulty:** n/a
- **Evaluate for:** how they handle technical disagreement, whether they
  changed their mind (or the reviewer's) based on evidence, not ego.

---

### Describe the hardest React bug you've debugged. How did you find the root cause?

- **Difficulty:** n/a
- **Evaluate for:** systematic debugging (bisecting, reading the actual
  stack/diff, forming and testing a hypothesis) vs. trial-and-error;
  whether they can explain *why* the fix worked, not just that it did.

---

### Tell me about a time you had to push back on a product/design requirement for technical reasons.

- **Difficulty:** n/a
- **Evaluate for:** communicating trade-offs to non-engineers, finding a
  workable middle ground vs. just saying no.

---

### Describe a time you inherited a messy React codebase or component. What did you do first?

- **Difficulty:** n/a
- **Evaluate for:** prioritization (tests before refactor? measure before
  optimizing?), whether they rewrote vs. incrementally improved, and why.

---

### Tell me about giving feedback on a junior teammate's PR that had a real problem.

- **Difficulty:** n/a
- **Evaluate for:** clarity + kindness balance, whether they explained the
  *why*, whether they left room for the author to still learn it themselves.
